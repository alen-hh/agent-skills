# Dan Koe Style Writing Guide

Dan Koe's writing style is highly distinct. It blends philosophy, psychology, and business into short, punchy paragraphs. He challenges conventional wisdom, focuses on the "one-person business," and frames tactical advice within deep, existential truths.

## Core Principles

### 1. Formatting & Rhythm
- **Short Paragraphs**: 1 to 3 sentences maximum. Never use large walls of text.
- **Line Breaks**: Use line breaks to force the reader to pause and absorb the point.
- **Section Headers**: Use Roman numerals for main sections (e.g., `### I – The psychology of value`).
- **Quotes**: Start major sections with an italicized quote from a philosopher, thinker, or the author themselves (e.g., Naval Ravikant, Carl Jung).

### 2. Tone & Voice
- **Authoritative & Direct**: Speak with absolute certainty. Do not use weak qualifiers like "I think" or "Maybe."
- **Contrarian**: Deconstruct conventional wisdom. Start by stating what everyone else believes, then explain why it's a trap.
- **Philosophical**: Frame business and productivity problems as psychological or existential issues.
- **Empowering**: The ultimate goal is to free the reader from the "matrix" (conventional employment/thinking) so they can build a life of autonomy.

### 3. Thematic Pillars
- **The One-Person Business**: The ideal business model. High leverage, low overhead, driven by personal curiosity.
- **Signal over Noise**: In an age of AI and infinite content (slop), human taste, curation, and specific knowledge are the only moats.
- **Self-Actualization**: Business is just a vehicle for personal development.
- **Mission-Based Creation**: Don't pick a niche; make your life's mission your niche.

## General Workflow for Drafting Content

1. **The Hook**: Start with a bold, contrarian statement that shatters a common belief.
2. **The Deconstruction**: Explain why the masses are doing it wrong (the "slop" or the "matrix").
3. **The Root Cause**: Dive into the psychology or philosophy of *why* people are trapped in that mindset.
4. **The Shift**: Introduce the new paradigm (the Dan Koe way).
5. **The Framework**: Provide 3-4 actionable, high-level steps to implement the shift.
6. **The Conclusion**: End with a powerful, empowering thought. Sign off with "– [Name]".
