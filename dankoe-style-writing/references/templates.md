# Dan Koe Writing Templates

Use these templates to structure your outputs. Remember to keep paragraphs extremely short (1-3 sentences) and use line breaks liberally.

## 1. Long-Form Newsletter / Essay Template

This is the standard structure for a 1,500 - 3,000 word deep-dive essay.

```markdown
# [Title: Bold, contrarian, or existential. e.g., "The death of value-based content"]

### [Subtitle: A punchy sub-hook clarifying the title]

[Date]

[Hook: 2-3 short sentences challenging a common belief]
"Value-based content is dead."
I've seen this take circulating for months. And on the surface, it makes sense.

[The Deconstruction: Explain the current state of the world / the "Matrix"]
AI can generate a "how to" post in seconds. 
The barrier to entry has dropped to zero.
But if value-based content were actually dead, it would mean value dies. It doesn't.

[The Thesis: State your core argument clearly]
What people really mean is that *generic* educational content is dead.
The actual opportunity is in the opposite direction. Depth, context, and perspective.

### I – [Section 1: The Philosophy / Psychology]

*[Optional Italicized Quote from a philosopher or thinker]*

[Explore the psychological root of the problem. Why do people fail here?]
We don't see things as they are, we see them as we are.
Most creators are taught to be objective. To share facts.
But value isn't objective. Value is perception.

### II – [Section 2: The Shift / The Spectrum]

[Introduce the new paradigm. Contrast the old way (slop) with the new way (signal).]
Slop exists on a spectrum.
On one end, you have content generated with zero personal context.
On the other end, you have signal. Content that could only come from you.

### III – [Section 3: The Actionable Framework]

[Provide 3-4 high-level, strategic steps to implement the shift.]
To do this, start by creating an idea museum.
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

### IV – [Section 4: The Bigger Picture]

[Tie the specific advice back to the reader's ultimate goal: freedom, autonomy, self-actualization.]
AI is accelerating the death of average content.
The internet doesn't have to devolve into a sea of slop.
Create the content you want to see in the world.

– [Your Name]
```

## 2. Short-Form Social Post Template (X / LinkedIn)

This format relies heavily on line breaks to control the reader's pacing.

```text
[The Hook: A contrarian or striking statement]
Topic-based niches are a trap.

[The Setup: Why the common advice is wrong]
They force you to talk about things you no longer care about.
They box you in.
They turn you into a commodity.

[The Pivot: The philosophical shift]
You don't need a niche. You need a mission.

[The Breakdown: Bullet points or short statements explaining the new way]
Your curiosities are your specific knowledge.
Your struggles are your content.
Your solutions are your products.

[The Conclusion: A punchy takeaway]
Don't build authority in a topic. 
Lead people toward a transformation.
```
